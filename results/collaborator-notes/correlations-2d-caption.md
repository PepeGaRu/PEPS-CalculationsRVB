We measure the connected physical bond covariance C_B(r)=<B_0 B_r>-<B_0><B_r>, B=S_i·S_j, using a D=3 PEPS and infinite-plane CTMRG contraction (χ=64). Boundary-contraction and sector-resolved transfer diagnostics follow the RVB-PEPS framework of Schuch et al. [1,2]; these are our model’s data. Over r=12–18, exponential and power-times-exponential fits give ξ≈0.82 and 0.86 simulation cells. The fitted pure-power exponent drifts from 13.4 to 18.1. Cutoff changes alter C_B(18) by 8%, so ξ≈0.8–0.9 is a finite-window decay scale, not a precise bulk asymptote. One translation follows a diagonal (1,−1) of the contracted square grid.
